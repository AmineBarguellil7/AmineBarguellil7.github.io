## Ownership

- `components/cinematic/cinematic-experience.tsx` loads WebGL only in the browser, skips it for reduced motion, and contains renderer failures without hiding content.
- `components/gsap-portfolio-effects.tsx` owns the DOM animations and ScrollTriggers. Section measurements feed one continuous chapter value. Match-media reversion cleans up animation styles and triggers; listeners and resize observation are removed on unmount.
- `components/cinematic/portfolio-world.tsx` owns the camera, procedural geometry, gradient surface textures, atmosphere shaders, starfield and scene transitions. Tunnel panels and rails are instanced. R3F disposes mounted geometry/materials; manually created textures and tube geometry have explicit disposal.
- `components/reveal.tsx` renders visible HTML before enhancement. Existing data, routes, anchors, CV and social destinations remain in their original components.

## Behavior and limits

Native scrolling and anchor navigation remain intact. Scene movement uses frame-rate-independent damping; the section measurements update when content or viewport dimensions change. Camera position adapts on smaller screens. Rendering pauses when the document is hidden, and pixel density is capped at 1.5. Reduced motion removes the WebGL canvas and cinematic heading spacers. Content remains visible when JavaScript or WebGL is unavailable.

All decorative geometry and textures are original and generated by code. The reference's branding, logos and proprietary models were not copied. This is a reconstruction of its scene progression and motion language, not an exact reproduction of its proprietary assets or recorded scroll timing.

## Validation

The initial browser comparison verified matching link destinations and section order; its only text difference was hero word spacing, which was corrected and checked separately. The portfolio data SHA-256 remained unchanged. Desktop (1440×960) and mobile (390×844) checks found no horizontal overflow or runtime errors. Mobile navigation closed and reached its target, the CV returned HTTP 200, reduced motion removed the canvas, and all five projects remained present without JavaScript. The production static export and targeted ESLint checks passed during implementation.
